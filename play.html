ⓘ How points work Up to 120 points for this word

Each correctly locked word earns 120 points with 1 clue. Every extra clue costs 20 points, down to a 20-point minimum.

1 clue 1202 clues 100 3 clues 804 clues 60 5 clues 406+ clues 20

Streak bonus: Each consecutive correct word adds 5 points to the next word, up to +30. An incorrect Lock attempt breaks that streak. Erasing extra AI letters doesn't directly reduce points; the clue count is what matters.

Coins are separate: Finished rounds normally award 20 coins, +10 for no failed locks, +5 for a streak of at least five, +15 for Daily, and +75 for each newly unlocked achievement. These wallet rewards are separate from score and may require a successfully saved round.
