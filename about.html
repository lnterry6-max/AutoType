ABOUT AUTOTYPE

A typing game that fights back.

AutoType flips the usual typing test around. You know the target sentence. The game predicts the rest of every word. Your job is to get the right answer out of the wrong guesses.

What makes it different?

Type a clue letter and AutoType fills in a matching word. If it's wrong, erase the extra letters, keep the correct ones and try another clue. When the word matches, lock it and keep going.

Each correct word earns points based on how efficiently you solved it. Different modes mix up the predictions, including context-aware guessing and intentionally tricky suggestions.
