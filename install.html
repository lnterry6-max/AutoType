AUTOTYPE FOR YOUR DEVICE

Your game, one tap away.

Put AutoType on your Home Screen and launch straight into the game—no browser tabs or app-store download needed.

AUTOTYPE · WEB APP
ADD TO HOME SCREEN

Feels like an app. Stays your AutoType.

Get a dedicated icon, a clean standalone window, and quick access to your games. Your account, friends and progress still use the same AutoType website.

Choose your device below to add AutoType to your Home Screen.

IPHONE & IPAD

Install from Safari

  1. Open auto-type.net in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (or the Page Menu, then Share).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.

The AutoType icon will appear on your Home Screen. Launch it from there to get the app-like window.

◈ANDROID

Install from Chrome

  1. Open auto-type.net in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm the installation and open AutoType from your launcher.

On supported browsers, the Install AutoType button above may open the install prompt directly.

GOOD TO KNOW

It's still a website—on purpose.

You'll need the internet to play and sync your account. If your connection drops, AutoType shows a simple offline screen instead of pretending old results are current. On some devices, the installed app may ask you to sign in again.
