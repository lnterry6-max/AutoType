Guide

How to Play

AutoType is not a normal typing test. The predictor physically inserts its guess, and you have to fight it when it's wrong.

THE CORE LOOP

One word, three decisions

TARGETminutes
→
YOU TYPEm
→
AUTOTYPE GUESSESmild
→
SMART ERASEmi ⌫ld
1

Type a clue letter

The target might be minutes. You type m.

2

AutoType commits to a word

It might guess mild. The i already matches the target, while ld does not.

3

Erase only the wrong tail

You only backspace ld. The correct i stays, so your usable prefix becomes mi. AutoType also tries not to repeat a recent guess when another completion is available.

4

Lock the correct word

Keep adding clues and rejecting only incorrect letters. When the visible word exactly matches the target, press Space or Enter.

Scoring

Points are earned for correctly locking words. A faster guess with fewer clue letters scores higher. Your streak adds a small bonus.

1 clue120
2 clues100
3 clues80
4 clues60
5 clues40
6+ clues20

Each consecutive correct word adds +5 points to the next, capped at +30. An incorrect Lock resets the streak. Erasing wrong autocomplete letters does not deduct points.

Coins are separate: Online completed rounds earn 20 coins, +10 for zero failed locks, +5 for a 5-word streak, +15 in Daily, and +75 for each new achievement. These rewards require a successfully saved round; score is not converted directly into coins.

* You can change confirm and erase shortcuts in Settings → Gameplay.
