CONTACT & SUPPORT

Need a hand?

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help with your AutoType account? Here are the ways to reach us during the beta.

FOR PLAYERS

Contact AutoType support

Questions, account problems or a game issue? Email our support address, or use the in-game feedback form when signed in.

Email support@auto-type.net
Use in-game feedback →

The in-game feedback form requires an AutoType account. Please don't send passwords, recovery codes or payment details.

PUBLIC BUG TRACKER

Report a technical issue

AutoType's GitHub repository provides a public place to report reproducible website issues. Don't post passwords, private messages, personal data or recovery links.

GitHub issues ↗

GitHub may require its own account to post.

ACCOUNT HELP

Locked out?

Use the account recovery flow to request a password reset. Signed-in players can change account settings or delete their account directly from Settings.

Account recoverySettings

Privacy or account-data requests

For questions about your personal information, account-data requests or privacy concerns, email privacy@auto-type.net. You can contact general support at support@auto-type.net, including when you can't sign in.

Please don't email passwords, payment numbers, security answers or recovery codes.
